04 / STRATEGIC PLANNING

SWOT, built into the concept.

Internal capabilities and limitations; external opportunities and threats.

↗ Strengths

  • Combines zoning, permit previews and recovery awareness.
  • Suggests alternatives instead of simply rejecting access.
  • What-if lab communicates environmental trade-offs.
  • One conceptual interface for citizens and authorities.

◷ Weaknesses

  • Recovery scores require validated field data.
  • Official permit integration is not available in this demo.
  • Maps need frequent updates and quality checks.
  • Reliable connectivity may be limited in remote areas.

✧ Opportunities

  • Potential partnerships with environmental authorities.
  • Growing demand for digital conservation tools.
  • Future use of satellite imagery and verified sensor data.
  • Alignment with national environmental sustainability goals.

⚑ Threats

  • Continued illegal activity despite digital guidance.
  • Cybersecurity and location-data privacy risks.
  • Changing regulations or limited data-sharing access.
  • Low adoption without community trust.
